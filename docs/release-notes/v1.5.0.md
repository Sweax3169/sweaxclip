**SweaxClip 1.5.0 — copy text from screen**

- **Text you can't select.** Press <kbd>Win</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>T</kbd>, drag a box over the text in a picture, a paused video, a shared slide, an error dialog or a game, and it is on your clipboard. The screen freezes while you select, every monitor works, and <kbd>Esc</kbd> or a right-click cancels. A note beside the box shows how many words were copied.
- **Read on your PC** by the Windows OCR engine, in the languages installed in Windows. Dark-mode text is cleaned up before reading.
- **Any shortcut you like.** Settings → General → *Copy text from screen*: click the shortcut and press your keys, or turn it off. Also in the tray menu and as `SweaxClip.exe --grab`.

| File | For |
| --- | --- |
| **`SweaxClip-win-x64.zip`** | Everyone. Extract and run `SweaxClip.exe`; nothing else to install. |
| `SweaxClip-lite.exe` | PCs that already have the [.NET 9 Desktop Runtime](https://dotnet.microsoft.com/download/dotnet/9.0). Smaller download. |

Updating: quit SweaxClip from the tray icon, then run the new `SweaxClip.exe`. Your history and settings are kept.

SmartScreen may warn because the executable is not code-signed: *More info → Run anyway*. Built from tag `v1.5.0` by the workflow in `.github/workflows/build.yml`; checksums are in `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

**Türkçe:** Ekrandan metin kopyalama geldi. <kbd>Win</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>T</kbd>'ye bas, ekran donar; bir görselin, duraklatılmış bir videonun, paylaşılan bir slaytın, bir hata penceresinin ya da bir oyunun içindeki yazının üstünden kutu sürükle, yazı panoya ve geçmişine düşer. Kutunun yanındaki not kaç kelime kopyalandığını gösterir; <kbd>Esc</kbd> ya da sağ tık iptal eder, her monitörde çalışır. Yazı bilgisayarında, Windows'un OCR motoruyla okunur. Kısayolu Ayarlar → Genel → *Ekrandan metin kopyala*'dan istediğin tuşlarla değiştirebilir ya da özelliği kapatabilirsin; tepsi menüsünde ve `SweaxClip.exe --grab` olarak da var.
